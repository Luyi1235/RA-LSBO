QUESTION
Does the already learned feasibility probability improve objective selection
when training data, objective model, feasible incumbent and candidate pool are
identical? This is a prospective new-data fixed-ranking diagnostic, not a new
optimization algorithm, online BO, qEI batch optimizer or safety method.

FOUR POLICIES, ONE FIT PER STATE
uniform_hash: outcome-independent salted hash ranking (one deterministic
uniform-like realization, not an exact uniform-random expectation).
risk_only: descending learned joint feasibility probability.
objective_ei: ordinary scalar Gaussian EI against best OBSERVED FEASIBLE target.
risk_ei: the SAME EI multiplied by the SAME learned joint feasibility probability.
No probability gate, tuned weight, information bonus or multi-step fantasy.
The conventional EI times feasibility product assumes independent objective/
constraint posteriors; it is an opt-in baseline, not an innovation claim.
No target is imputed for region-aborted designs. Exact objective values from
completed-but-resource-infeasible training designs remain legitimate labels.
Objective GP uses all available exact objective labels and original
StableGroupedHead(objective=True,reference_scale=1), frozen 4x2 kernel grid.
Risk uses unmodified native_censored. Features are explicit, no VAE.
All policies share the objective predictions AND normalization exactly.

WARM/COLD
Primary analysis is initially-feasible (warm) states. Do not reject/resample
cold groups. Without feasible incumbent, objective_ei and risk_ei both use
the identical uniform_hash ordering; risk_only remains a separate reference.
These cold groups do NOT test objective EI or online first-feasible learning.
