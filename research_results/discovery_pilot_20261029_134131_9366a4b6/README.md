# Discovery Pilot, 2026-10-29

This is an exploratory mathematical study, not an independent confirmation or an aircraft MDA experiment. The run used commit `cfdf7c30216585b3920f23723236708859435ce7`, two sparse cases, 20 seeds and five methods. It settled 6,516 of 6,516 registered mathematical calls, with no RCAIDE MDA calls. The preceding discovery smoke settled 104 calls.

The recorded audit passed for 200 trials and 6,000 proposals; a subsequent read-only audit reproduced the saved audit. It checks evidence and policy invariants but does not replay the optimizer or establish statistical superiority. Both the cold-start success denominators and conditional feasible-objective limitations must be retained when interpreting the results.

`report.md`, `summary.csv`, `results.json`, `audit.json`, `plan.json`, and `registration.json` are original generated files.
