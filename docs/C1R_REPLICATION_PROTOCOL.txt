1. Scientific question and freeze
The positive C1 comparison motivates an independent fresh-seed replication,
not an additional tuned acquisition function. Keep exactly the same four
known tradeoff tasks, six arms, initial48, pool256, online24, GP models, kernel
grid, EI, feasible incumbent updates, censored feedback and fallback rules.
Run the unmodified c1_study engine. Only its plan provider and the verifier's
plan provider are scoped to the C1R plan. Old source files are unchanged.
No evaluation of held-out s4-s7; this is NOT held-out-task confirmation.
No new algorithm novelty or safety guarantee is claimed.

Arms: ei_frozen, risk_ei_frozen, ei_updated, risk_ei_updated,
risk_only_updated, uniform_hash.
Main comparison: risk_ei_updated vs risk_ei_frozen.
Endpoint: final best feasible objective after 24 calls, initial_warm only,
separately for each of tradeoff_s0,s1,s2,s3. Lower is better.
Failures, exact-objective yield, branches and computation costs are reported
alongside target improvement, not hidden or turned into an unregistered penalty.
All cold groups retained. No replacement seeds, no resampling to obtain labels.

3. Predefined statistical report
For each of four tasks, report all seed-level objective differences, mean,
median and W/L/T. Ties use the original tolerance 1e-10*max(1,abs(a),abs(b)).
Primary directional test is the two-sided exact binomial sign test on non-ties.
No discordances gives p=1. Apply Holm adjustment across exactly four tasks,
family-wise alpha=.05. This tests win/loss direction, NOT the mean effect size.
A rejection in the opposite direction is adverse, not success. Always report
sign, raw effects, ties, failures and sample counts. Absence of rejection is
inconclusive, not proof of equivalence. No pooling with the eight pilot seeds.
No cross-task raw objective average. Other contrasts and k6/k12 are descriptive,
not additional independent samples or new primary tests.
The same report can summarize the old pilot, but confirmatory=false there.
With registered model failures, keep the predefined fallback trajectories in
analysis; no selective removal. No assumed calibrated real-world failure cost.
