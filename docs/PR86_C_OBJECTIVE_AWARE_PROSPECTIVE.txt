1. PRIMARY SCIENTIFIC QUESTION
Does acquisition weighted toward objective opportunity AND local feasibility uncertainty improve the first observed exact feasible objective, compared to:
IID, joint feasibility boundary sampling, marginal constraint entropy, and standard constrained EI?
Do NOT call this an exact global VOI, full knowledge gradient, or valid safety guarantee.

2. FROZEN MATHEMATICAL EVALUATION CONTRACT
Mixed candidate: branch=0..3, x/y in [0,1], trim=0.5 when branch0, else trim in [0,1].
Native mixed-structure features, not a newly trained VAE.
Two constraints: region<=0 AND resource<=0; joint probability is marginal product approximation.
Observed region: numeric exact when region<=0, else only strict g_region>0 interval.
Observed resource: numeric exact every evaluation.
Observed objective: exact only if region<=0, else missing due to controlled region abort.
Objective GP fits ONLY exact objective observations, INCLUDING resource-infeasible but region-valid objective values (not privileged).
A design counts as an exact feasible objective only when both observed constraints <=0 AND objective has an exact value.
Simulator-completion risk is not separately modeled. Every analytic truth evaluation settles without simulated numerical crash.

3. SAME-BUDGET COMPARISON
Methods:
 (A) iid_random: random unseen pool point.
 (B) boundary: maximize p_joint*(1-p_joint).
 (C) marginal_entropy: maximize H(p_region)+H(p_resource) WITHOUT 25% random calibration reservation; isolates PR86-B's marginal entropy score from the reservation confound.
 (D) constrained_ei: maximize p_joint*EI(f_best) for minimization, provided there is an exact feasible incumbent and trained objective GP.
 (E) objective_weighted_boundary: rank score = 0.65*percentile_rank(p_joint*EI)+0.35*percentile_rank(EI*H(p_joint)); ranks average ties. Candidate is a local objective-relevance heuristic NOT true expected VOI.

In the absence of a known exact feasible incumbent OR trained objective GP, set EI=1 for the two objective methods. That means D chooses highest p_joint, and E uses 0.65 rank(p_joint)+0.35 rank(H(p_joint)).
No model-based calibration for any arm; all online acquisition decisions use raw GP probabilities.
All arms share same 12 random initial observations. Each chooses 36 additional paid observations from same independent candidate pool, without replacement. GP fits after every actual outcome (plus final checkpoint). Heldout truth must NEVER be exposed to the optimizer or objective GP.
