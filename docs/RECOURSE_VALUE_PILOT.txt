Question and exact intervention
For first candidate x and observable outcome O, let v(y,O) be the second-call
one-step score after the SAME EP fantasy update, on the SAME actual next pool.
Cold feasible first outcomes have v=0 because delivery has been achieved.
Q_adaptive(x) = Q1(x) + sum_w max_y v(y,O).
Q_fixed(x)    = Q1(x) + max_y sum_w v(y,O).
Their finite-node difference >=0 follows algebraically; this alone is NOT an
empirical information benefit, calibrated probability, or safety statement.
Only changing the order of max and averaging removes outcome-specific second
choice in the acquisition, without deleting observations or changing GP heads.
All genuine future observations are used and next calls are replanned in BOTH
arms. 'fixed_second' means fixed WITHIN THE SCORING CALCULATION, not execution
of a stale commitment. This is not a nonadaptive batch optimizer comparison.
